Hello! I am a first year oceanography PhD student at Rutgers University's [Haskin Shellfish Research Laboratory](https://hsrl.rutgers.edu/), advised by [Dr. Mike Acquafredda](https://acquafreddalab.rutgers.edu/). I am interested in researching prospects for restoration aquaculture using the ribbed mussel (_Geukensia demissa_) for nutrient bioextraction, bacterial remediation, and shoreline stabilization.

I earned my AS in Environmental Science from Raritan Valley Community College, and my BS in Marine Science from Rutgers University. As an undergraduate, I was a [NOAA Hollings Scholar](https://www.noaa.gov/office-education/hollings-scholarship), and dabbled in many areas of research, from aquaculture science to astrobiology to biogeochemistry.
